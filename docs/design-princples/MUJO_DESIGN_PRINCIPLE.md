## In one sentence

**Mujō (無常, "impermanence")** is the principle that the observer — human or agent — is a **transient,
conditioned instrument**: its context is ephemeral (the session dies) and its lived experience
(newness, prior exposure, cognitive style) makes it **reliably miss a specific, non-random set of
things** — so truth must be made durable, emitted *during* the work, and re-observed by a
*differently-situated* observer. Its remedy is the **Five Continuity Signals** (STATE · ACCOMPLISHMENTS
· DISCOVERIES · NEXT ACTIONS · **WARNINGS/negative-knowledge — read first**), emitted during the
session, not after (design rule #1). See the canonical document for the full principle.

Sits next to **Personal Conway** (the observer's *stable* fingerprint; Mujō is its *transient* twin) in
the Reflection & meta cluster.
